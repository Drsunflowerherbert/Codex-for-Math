## Highest-risk correspondence distinctions

- **BSD:** family002 requires full Selmer corank zero or one at a prime. Rank alone is insufficient; this is not unrestricted BSD. The exact leading term, rank converse and two-primary factor are distinct statements.
- **Riemann:** family003 gives a claimed fixed zero-free half-plane, not full RH. The Siegel-zero entry excludes only a logarithmic neighborhood of 1.
- **Hodge:** family032 contains restricted CM, K3-locus and generic-family claims, a conditional Kuga–Satake reduction, and three withdrawn unrestricted claims. Those flags are manuscript-specific.
- **Hilbert16:** the primary general uniform-bound statement is different from the quintic Liénard theorem documented by the family Lean scope note. Neither gives an effective general B(d) here.
- **π and Flint–Hills:** the source `main` declaration and its definitions were inspected, but proof imports were not checked. `flint_hills_summable` exists as a separate source declaration and is outside the comparator-selected `main` statement. Our tangent libraries still assume their π premise.
- **Crouzeix and Tingley:** scoped positive-block, convex-approximation and radial-contact libraries in this repository supply tools under their own contracts; they do not prove the original full headline claims.
- **Forced fluids:** family376 designs external forcing to encode computation. Its periodic/stationary/decaying realizations must remain separate; it is not the unforced Navier–Stokes regularity problem.
