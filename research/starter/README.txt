MATHEMATICS RESEARCH STARTER — HISTORICAL INPUTS
Prepared 11 October 2026; public snapshot edition.

This bundle preserves initial reusable studies and a revision-pinned corpus
inventory. Current corrected affine/Egyptian studies are in ../supplement;
use ../README.md and ../supplement/STUDY-INDEX.json as the research entry points.

The pi-bridge and pi-series proofs are conditional ordinary mathematics with
explicit hypotheses and a separate ordinary review. The radial study has
its own ordinary review. These files contain no Lean verification of the studies.
The standalone Lean projects elsewhere in this repository have separate specs,
compiler evidence, and limitations. Exact computations are finite checks.

The corpus inventory records OpenAI/math revision
fd4aeeb2ee4fc729c18d98444fed42fd0529eeeb: 719 active manuscripts, 372 families,
three withdrawn manuscripts and 27 directory-version changes. Manuscript,
family, formalization-source and declaration counts are different units.
Upstream claims and metadata are not independently certified here. Source
URLs and fingerprints are preserved. Third-party attribution/license coverage
is explained in ../../THIRD-PARTY.md.

JSON theorem records are portable provisional research data. No native MRS
schema validation, import, external peer-review certification or novelty claim
is made. Obsolete affine/Egyptian notes are historical review inputs, not current
mathematical entry points. Optional ordinary Egyptian coarse/iterated bounds
are separately unreviewed.

MANIFEST-SHA256.supplied.json preserves the original archive hashes. Publication
sanitization/exclusions and current hashes are in ../../publication/SOURCE-MAP.json
and ../../publication/FILES.json. Use python3 ../../publication/verify.py for
current snapshot integrity. The research-launch prompt is excluded.
