# Publication review

Two independent automated reviewers inspected the four initial archives and
the later sharp tangent extension. The initial privacy review covered all
276 candidates; the sharp review covered all 128 archive entries, including
the redundant patch. Reviewers inspected text, JSON, scripts, Lean source,
source hashes and supplied receipts without executing archived programs.
These are scoped publication reviews, not external peer-review certification.

The mathematical-status review independently confirmed:

- Radial: 47 declarations, matching source and supplied full statement/axiom
  coverage, correct pins and 27 supplied regression tests.
- Egyptian: 42 theorems among 46 named declarations, matching supplied source
  hashes and reviews, finite-state termination orientation and marker contracts.
- Ordinary supplement: 81 core records across seven studies, corrected affine
  and Egyptian entry points, no Lean verification of those ordinary records,
  and separately unreviewed optional Egyptian coarse/iterated bounds.
- Sharp tangent: 78 declarations, including two mathematical private lemmas;
  matching public/private audit coverage; explicit PiDiophantine for sufficiency;
  unconditional divergence for s ≤ 1. All original modules and pins match the
  requested base. Only imports and the inventory were extended in this separate
  project copy.

No source proof admissions, custom axioms, native-evaluation trust changes,
unexpected axiom dependencies, pin discrepancy or checked original receipt
mismatch was identified. Final audits allow only `propext`, `Classical.choice`
and `Quot.sound`. Earlier failed compilation diagnostics remain historical.

Privacy inspection found no actual credential, personal email, private
screenshot or signed download URL in the source bundles. Private artifact
identifiers, transfer routes and internal coordinator wording were removed;
historical paths were normalized. Launch instructions, download/cache
diagnostics, a nested binary checkpoint, a private packaging helper and the
redundant sharp patch were excluded. The public source map records original
and sanitized hashes without private contents.

Redistribution review found a pinned upstream Apache 2.0 license and no extra
restriction in retained source snapshots. The license and source attribution
are included. Derived inventory/excerpt changes are explicitly identified.
No license was assigned to new original work.

Publication-session compiler and computation results are separate from supplied
receipts. Current hashes use `FILES.json`; supplied nested review/manifest
hashes are historical. Later starter retrieval preserves the formalizers'
historical input-access limits and does not certify all ordinary/formal source
equivalences. The original tangent tree is checked byte-for-byte against the
base commit before publication.

The final assembled-tree reviews passed after documentary corrections. The
privacy reviewer checked every current manifest file, all JSON/Python syntax,
no unmanifested publishable files, source-map dispositions, license notices,
and original tangent preservation. The mathematical-status reviewer inspected
the fresh compiler output and receipt consistency: radial 47 declarations and
27 regression tests; Egyptian 42 theorem axiom records and exact checks; sharp
76 public plus two private declarations; ordinary full reproduction 12 commands.
No additional mathematical, privacy or redistribution blocker remains for this
five-bundle publication. Supplied archival end-of-file blank lines are retained
for byte provenance; whitespace diagnostics on those original inputs are
documented rather than changing source snapshots.
