## Statements and assumptions

The principal application is an equality between actual Lebesgue integral
columns. For `P,D : Polynomial ℚ`, natural `L,j`, contact
`t*P(t)/sqrt(1-t²)-D(t)=O(t^L)` at zero, and `j<L`, it gives explicit
rational witnesses `A,B` for the corrected integral `A+B*Constants.catalan`.
Both the positive-side filter `𝓝[>] 0` and the two-sided filter `𝓝 0` have
declared application theorems. The source definitions of these columns are
integrals, and their integrability, recurrence data, and Catalan/zeta boundary
starts are proved separately. The final applications do not assume their own
decomposition or boundary evaluations.

The reviewer checked the source types and the complete printed kernel types
of all nine application declarations. The rational domain, exact filters, and
strict column condition remain visible. The free coordinate space
`Fin 3 → ℚ` and its real evaluation are distinct; evaluation is not assumed
injective. A nonzero designated zeta coordinate proves no arithmetic
nonmembership. There is no Catalan irrationality or arithmetic independence
claim.

All 21 original theorem records were compared with the declaration coverage
table. The table retains these incomplete clauses: arbitrary real-polynomial
interfaces; global analytic summation of the inverse-square-root generating
series; the optional pi/Basel value; general contact for every analytic
function; the final actual-integral two-polynomial cancellation bridge;
uniqueness and divisibility classification for contact truncations; the tail
endpoint `i=0,N=1` and a named actual-integral tail specialization; and
arbitrary coefficient-subfield extension. The tail theorem expressly requires
`2≤i+N`, nonnegativity, its recurrence, and decay. These limits remain part of
the accepted scope. Mapping all records does not claim every clause formalized.
