# Ghys source-audit search log

Search date: 16 October 2026. Searches were read-only; no author, publisher, or other person was contacted. No review material was published.

## Primary pages checked

- [Ghys's publication list](https://perso.ens-lyon.fr/ghys/papers/): links the original article PDF and its review. No linked erratum was visible beside the article.
- [Original article DOI/publisher page](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/BF01245193): article identity and references verified. No correction notice was visible in the accessible page.
- [Ghys original author's PDF](https://perso.ens-lyon.fr/ghys/articles/holomorphicanosov.pdf): proofs at pp. 589–598 inspected; rendered pp. 592, 593, and 596 were also checked to resolve OCR/symbol ambiguity.
- [Cantat 2004 official EMS PDF](https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/43027): Proposition 1.2 and §5.1 corroborate the holomorphic-foliation use of Ghys Proposition 2.2. This is corroboration of the interpretation, not independent proof verification.
- [Zhang's 2025 DCDS article page](https://www.aimsciences.org/article/doi/10.3934/dcds.2024102) and [author preprint v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2311.00251v2): §4 uses the weaker correct combinatorial Fact 1. It assumes transitivity and is not an erratum.
