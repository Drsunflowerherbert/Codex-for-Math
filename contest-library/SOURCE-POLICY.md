# Source, access and publication policy

Only past contest editions through 2025 are eligible for this collection.
The 2026 Schweitzer feedback program requests work without AI; its current
problem content and submissions are excluded. No current-round problem files
are opened or analyzed. A past archive ZIP can contain a 2026 filename; discovering
that name is not problem coverage.

Official organizer pages and organizer-hosted papers are the primary sources.
Organizer hosting does not by itself make a solution official: labels in the
paper take priority. Paper identity is the original year, competition stage,
subject, category and problem number. Editorial summaries do not replace the
original assumptions. A suspected error is recorded separately and never repaired
silently in the index.

Each downloaded source record includes its source URL, UTC retrieval date/time,
SHA-256 digest, and where applicable original archive member name and page range.
Hashes identify the retrieved bytes, not a guarantee that a future download will
match. These private research copies are kept outside the Git worktree, under
`/tmp/contest-library-research/`; they are not part of the published collection.
A path mentioned in a source record describes a local research copy only.

No explicit permission to redistribute full contest papers was located in the
inspected archive pages. This edition therefore publishes links, metadata,
short original topic descriptions, and our original proof/hint/review notes.
It does not publish third-party PDFs, extracted paper text, scanned pages or
full problem compilations. No overall license is assigned to those sources or
to the repository by this edition. Organizer copyright and source labels remain
with their owners. No special reuse permission is inferred from public access.

The default problem index and practice path contain neither proofs nor hints.
Official solution links and authored notes have separate opt-in pages. Where
an archive document combines problems and solutions, the default index links to
the archive landing page rather than opening that combined document directly.

## Status vocabulary

- **Located and retrieved:** a specific solution document was identified and
  its bytes successfully retrieved; its official or unofficial status is explicit.
- **Located, access blocked:** a specific document is linked or indexed, but
  retrieval failed. This is an access limitation.
- **Not located in bounded inspection:** the inspected sources did not yield
  an identified official solution. This does not establish that none exists.
- **Outside scope:** discovered material was not extracted or checked for this edition.
- **Reviewed ordinary proof:** an original draft was read by a different agent,
  checked against the source and mathematical argument, and any reported concerns
  resolved before publication. This is human-readable proof review, not a proof
  assistant certificate.

The publication branch is `codex/contest-library-2026-10-13`, based on verified
research baseline `e4f337c3aa52ec4c3f2aab078d7c91b32ee0015f`.
Only `contest-library/` is changed. The prior worktree and branches are preserved.
No main-branch update, PR, merge or force push is part of this task.
