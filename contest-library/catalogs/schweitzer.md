# Miklós Schweitzer archive — first edition

This catalog links the organizer’s archive without copying its papers. Retrieval: 13 October 2026 UTC. Topics, prerequisites, and difficulty are editorial judgments; difficulty levels follow [the shared taxonomy](../TAXONOMY.md). Read each exact original statement in its official paper; numbering and original assumptions control.

Coverage: **17 years (2009–2025)**; **171 statement numbers observed**, plus **11 problems reported for 2012 whose statements were not located**. **42 problems from 2022–2025** have individual topic entries. Years before 2009 and all 2026 program problems/submissions are excluded.

The 2026 feedback program asks participants to avoid AI. This edition is archived practice only. The current [organizer hub](https://www.bolyai.hu/versenyek-schweitzer-miklos-emlekverseny/) supplies the archive and notice.
