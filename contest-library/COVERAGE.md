topic entries. Those inventories are not added to the detailed-entry total.

## Availability and missing coverage

Yau's official archive contains specific Analysis solution papers for 2020–2022,
covering all six problems per year. Other indexed Analysis official solutions
were not located in the bounded archive/domain inspection. The three authored
attempts concern 2014 individual P1, 2019 individual P1 and 2023 P4; all have
accepted independent ordinary-proof reviews. Their hints and proofs are separate.

Schweitzer has **160 observed organizer/committee solution numbers** and **11
explicitly unofficial 2023 solution numbers** in the retrieved papers. The
2022 and 2024 answers are preliminary versions. The 2012 link is a results-only
report; actual statements and answers remain not located. Years before 2009 and
detailed classification before 2022 are outside this edition. Source-reported
wording warnings for 2023/8 and 2025/3 are separately qualified.

Jarník's overview shows Category II problem links for **1997–2019, 2022, 2024,
2025** (26 years) and solution links for **2007–2019, 2022, 2024, 2025** (16 years).
For 1992–1996 only Category II results rows were observed; no Category II row was
observed for 1991. Editions 2020, 2021 and 2023 were not listed in the inspected
overview. This does not establish cancellation or absence of other archives.
Earlier detailed problems and complete PDF fidelity remain missing.

IMC inventories all 32 year landing pages, with 89 distinct linked PDFs receiving
successful HTTP HEAD checks. Only 2023–2025 questions and answers were downloaded
(12 PDFs). The older 1994–2022 individual problem numbering and topics were not
extracted. A successful HEAD check confirms access to headers at that time,
not complete-paper retrieval or mathematical inspection.

All source URLs, UTC retrieval times/dates, language qualifications and hashes
for downloaded bytes are in the collector JSON. The primary archives are
[Yau](https://yau-contest.com/en/lists-jxxg.html),
[Schweitzer](https://www.bolyai.hu/versenyek-schweitzer-miklos-emlekverseny/),
[Jarník](https://jarnik.osu.cz/problems) and
[IMC](https://www.imc-math.org.uk/?item=problems&year=2025).
No search failure is treated as proof that an official solution does not exist.
Current/restricted 2026 problem content and submissions are excluded throughout.
